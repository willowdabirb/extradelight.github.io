Melting Pot

The Melting Pot melts solids into liquids.

Usage

Stick items inside to convert them into their liquid forms. The Melting Pot must be above a heat source block to function.

Crafting

Added in

2.1

Used to make

  • Liquid Blood Chocolate
  • Liquid Dark Chocolate
  • Liquid Milk Chocolate
  • Liquid White Chocolate
  • Marshmallow Fluff
  • Water
