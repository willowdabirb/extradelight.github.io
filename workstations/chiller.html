Chiller

The Chiller chills a range of icy treats - solidifying chocolate, jelly, ice cream, etc.! It takes in a fluid, up to 4 ingredients, a container, and something to chill everything down. If you have all the correct ingredients but nothing is happening... you might need to empty the drip-tray!

Usage

To use the Chiller, you can insert liquid to freeze in the top left bucket slot and take it out from the bottom left. As items freeze, they will leave behind water in the drip-tray; collect it by putting a bucket in the bottom right slot.

Ice or something cold is required for freezing, and can be inserted in the topmost box above the snowflake on the right. From shortest- to longest-lasting freezing durability, you can use ice cubes, snowballs, snow blocks, ice blocks, packed ice, or blue ice.

Ice blocks can be downgraded into 9 blocks of the next lowest level by chopping them on a cutting board with an axe, and can be recrafted again.
